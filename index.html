Featured — Color Snake

A maze snake puzzle where you mix colors by eating orbs and match the exit hue. Cute rounded snake, local progress, no ads, no accounts.

Color Snake Privacy Closed testing link

How to play

  1. Swipe or drag to steer the snake through the maze.
  2. Eat colored orbs to mix your snake’s color.
  3. Match the exit’s color, then reach the exit to clear the level.
  4. Later mazes get tighter — plan your path and your mix.

Mechanics

  • Color mixing: eating orbs tints the snake so it can match the exit.
  • Exit match: wrong hue won’t clear — mix until it fits.
  • Puzzle mazes: walls and layout force careful routes.
  • Local save: progress stays on your device.
  • Privacy-first: no ads, no accounts, no tracking SDKs.
